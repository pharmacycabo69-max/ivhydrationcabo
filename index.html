Good to Know

Answers before you ask.

Who actually administers the IV?+

A licensed nurse, every time. We don't send unlicensed staff to place an IV line, regardless of how simple the drip is.

How fast can someone get here?+

Most visits arrive within 45–60 minutes across Cabo San Lucas and the Corridor. San José del Cabo may run slightly longer depending on traffic.

Is this safe if I've been drinking?+

Our nurses assess vitals before starting any drip and will advise if a drip isn't appropriate for your situation.

Do you take walk-in requests same day?+

Yes — most bookings are same-day. Message the concierge chat or WhatsApp and we'll confirm the next available nurse.

Still not sure what you need?

Answer a couple of quick questions and the concierge will point you to the right drip — no account, no app.
